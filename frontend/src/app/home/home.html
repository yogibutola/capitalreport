Smart Slotting™

Balanced matchups, generated in one tap.

Smart Slotting reads DUPR, availability, and who has already played whom, then fills every court with pairings that are actually competitive — so nobody sits, and nobody gets rolled.

  • Targets a ±0.15 DUPR spread on every court.
  • Respects availability, no-repeat partners, and your real court count.
  • Someone drops out? Re-slot the night in one tap.
